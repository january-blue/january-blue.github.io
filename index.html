I am currently an M.Eng. student in TMI, School of Engineering, The University of Tokyo. My earlier academic training includes a B.S. in Mathematical Economics from Fudan University and a brief period in Georgia Tech's OMSCS program. Prior to graduate study, I spent three years working on data-driven growth strategy at a FinTech SaaS company.

I am interested in how general intelligent systems, from humans to language models, reason, and how such reasoning can be evaluated with care. My recent work has touched on LLM evaluation, logic-and-NLP benchmarks, and AI systems for scholarly workflows.
